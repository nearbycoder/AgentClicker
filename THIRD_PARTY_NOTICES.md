# Third-party notices

Agent Clicker's models, scene, audio, writing and trailer are original to this project. The 3D models are
generated by the Python scripts in `Blender/scripts`, and every sound effect and the music are synthesised at
runtime by `Unity/Assets/Scripts/Util/Sfx.cs`. The game ships with no audio files. All labs, AI models,
companies and people in the game are fictional.

The repository and the release builds also contain the following third-party material.
